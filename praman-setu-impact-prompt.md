Create a clean, presentation-ready impact infographic for **Praman Setu - Digital Companion for Field Drug Testing**, SIH26231.

- Use a wide 16:9 canvas, approximately 1600 x 1000 pixels.
- Use a transparent or black background.
- Center the heading at the top:
  - `Praman Setu`
  - `Driving Reliable Field Evidence`
- Place three equal-height vertical rounded panels below the heading with generous spacing.
- Use a consistent icon above each impact point and keep all text short enough to fit comfortably.
- Use a clean, modern government-technology infographic style.
- Use bold white panel headings and high-contrast body text.
- Do not use gradients, photographs, decorative clutter, or drug imagery.
- Use a thin colored border around each panel and a matching accent color for its icons.
