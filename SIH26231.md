## Problem and Product Boundary

Field colorimetric drug-testing kits are currently interpreted visually. Praman Setu makes that interpretation more consistent and creates a verifiable digital record by using the device camera, a reference color card, device metadata, and cryptographic integrity controls.

The application works alongside existing test kits and does not require new hardware. Its output is a **presumptive field-test result and supporting digital record**. It does not replace laboratory confirmatory testing.

## Prototype Goals

1. Capture a test image with a reference color card in frame.
2. Calibrate the image and classify the result as Preliminary Positive, Preliminary Negative, or Inconclusive.
3. Record timestamp, GPS location, operator ID, kit metadata, timing, quality information, and image hash.
4. Store evidence offline and provide a searchable history.
5. Make records tamper-evident with a hash chain and device-key signature.
6. Demonstrate verification, export, retesting, and supervisor review.

## In-Scope Prototype Features

### Capture and classification

- Guided camera capture with reference-card and test-kit overlays.
- Exposure and white-balance lock when aligned.
- Manual marking of six reference patches: white, black, red, green, blue, and gray.
- White-patch gain correction and reference-card validation.
- Sample-region selection with adjustable radius.
- Median RGB to LAB conversion and Delta E classification.
- Confidence score and an Inconclusive threshold.
- Capture quality score from 0 to 100, with Good (80-100), Fair (60-79), and Poor (below 60).
- Hard quality gates for severe blur, strong reaction-area glare, and invalid reference cards.
- Structured inconclusive reasons and recommended next actions.

### Evidence and integrity

- Original, uncorrected captured image is hashed with SHA-256.
- Record contains timestamp, GPS, operator ID, kit details, case ID when provided, timing, quality, calibration, result, confidence, Delta E, reasons, retake count, retest link, and reference database version.
- Each record stores the previous record hash, its own record hash, and a signature from a device key pair.
- Original test records are immutable in the application model.
- Export contains JSON and image; PDF embeds the image, metadata, hash, and QR code.
- Verification checks image hash, signature validity, and chain integrity.

### Kit and workflow controls

- QR scan reads kit ID, kit type, manufacturer, batch/lot, manufacture date, and expiry date.
- Scanned kit type locks the kit selector.
- Valid kits continue; expired or unrecognized kits block testing.
- Reaction timer uses the required interval from the validated kit protocol and is not editable by the officer.
- Capture is locked during the countdown and enabled during the capture window.
- Capture outside a maximum window forces an Inconclusive result.
- Retest creates a separate linked record using `retest_of`; records are compared side by side and never merged.

### Local operation and review

- Every test is saved to SQLite before synchronization.
- Sync statuses: PENDING, SYNCED, and FAILED, with retry support.
- History is newest first and supports date, officer, result, kit, and case filters.
- Supervisor review is read-only and can set REVIEWED, REQUIRES RETEST, or REQUIRES LAB CONFIRMATION.
- Supervisor actions do not modify the original test record.

## Screen Flow

1. **Login:** badge ID and PIN; optional biometric mock; operator ID and role enter session.
2. **Home:** operator identity, Start New Test, View History, and sync status.
3. **Kit Select and QR Scan:** select or scan kit, validate metadata and expiry, optionally enter case/FIR and witness IDs.
4. **Reaction Timer:** show kit protocol and enforce the capture interval.
5. **Guided Capture:** align reference card and test kit, run quality gates, capture image.
6. **Mark Reference Patches:** tap six patches, calibrate, and validate the card.
7. **Mark Sample Region:** tap reaction area, inspect ROI quality, and calculate color values.
8. **Result:** show result, confidence, Delta E, quality, card, kit, timing, metadata, reasons, and recommendation.
9. **Save and Export:** persist, hash, chain, sign, export PDF/JSON/image, and return Home.
10. **History:** search, filter, view read-only details, delete with chain warning, or repeat a test.
11. **Retest Comparison:** show two independent linked records side by side.
12. **Verify:** upload JSON and image to `verify.html`; show hash, signature, and chain status.
13. **Supervisor Review:** read-only submitted record review and review status.

## Data and Classification Rules

- The original image used for SHA-256 must remain unchanged; calibration is applied only to the analysis copy.
- Reference-card validation must check patch completeness and frame position, expected hue, white clipping, black crushing, neutral black-gray-white ramp, calibration residual Delta E, and recognized card version.
- Strong glare on the reaction area is a hard retake gate. Mild glare reduces quality and may result in Inconclusive.
- A uniformly pale or clear sample is not automatically glare.
- Inconclusive reasons may include blur, poor lighting, excessive glare, small reaction area, multiple colors, card calibration failure, measurement uncertainty, reaction outside the reference range, and capture outside the timing window.
- The app must recommend whether to retake the photograph, repeat the field test, or preserve the sample for laboratory confirmation.

## Demo and Safety Constraints

- Demonstration uses simulation only: no drugs and no real test kit.
- Every demo material and screen must be visibly labeled: **SIMULATED - NOT A DRUG TEST**.
- Use printed color swatches, simulated pouches, printed results, colored water in labeled vials, or a before/after visual simulation.
- Do not label a simulated color as a specific drug or claim a drug identification percentage.
- Demo wording should say simulated preliminary result based on color matching.
- The app and pitch must clearly state that field results are presumptive and require laboratory confirmation.

### Legal-alignment checkpoints shown in the reference video

- **BNSS Section 105:** the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita provides for audio-video recording of search and seizure processes. Praman Setu may support a time-linked capture package and record metadata, but it does not replace the authorized officer's statutory recording, forwarding, or procedural duties.
- **NDPS Act Section 52A:** seizure documentation includes inventory, photographs, representative samples, and the applicable Magistrate-certification process. Praman Setu should record checklist completion and certificate references; the app cannot perform Magistrate certification or replace laboratory analysis.
- **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam Section 63(4):** electronic-record production may require a prescribed certificate and source/device particulars. A hash or digital signature alone must not be presented as automatic compliance. The export should provide a certificate-ready evidence bundle for the competent authority.
- **Yusuf @ Asif v. State:** the reference video highlights Section 52A compliance and the consequences of treating procedural safeguards as optional. This is a legal review checkpoint, not a claim that the app decides admissibility.
- **Implementation implication:** add a legal-compliance checklist with fields for recording availability, seizure/inventory reference, photograph reference, sample/certificate reference, responsible officer, and review status. Keep these fields separate from the presumptive color classification.

### Forensic and drug-analysis guidance

- [NIST Forensic Science](https://www.nist.gov/forensic-science): research, standards, validation, quality, and evidence-handling context.
- [NIST OSAC Forensic Science Standards](https://www.nist.gov/adlp/spo/organization-scientific-area-committees-forensic-science): technically sound forensic standards and guidance; use as a quality reference, not as Indian law.
- [NIST OSAC Seized Drugs Process Map](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2022/11/osacs-seized-drugs-subcommittee-develops-process-map): process-oriented reference for seized-drug examination decisions and documentation.
- [SWGDRUG Approved Recommendations](https://www.swgdrug.org/approved.htm): international forensic drug-analysis recommendations and terminology reference; not a Government of India regulation.
- [ISO/IEC 17025:2017](https://www.iso.org/standard/66912.html): competence, impartiality, and consistent operation of testing and calibration laboratories. It applies to laboratory quality, not direct approval of this field prototype.

### Digital evidence and security references

- [NIST FIPS 180-4 - Secure Hash Standard](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/180-4/upd1/final): reference for cryptographic hash functions and change detection.
- [NIST FIPS 186-5 - Digital Signature Standard](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/186-5/final): reference for digital-signature algorithms and signer authentication.
- [ISO/IEC 27001:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001): information-security risk-management reference for future production controls; the prototype is not ISO-certified.

### How the sources affect Praman Setu

- The app uses a presumptive-result boundary and routes uncertainty or out-of-range results toward repeat testing or laboratory confirmation.
- The workflow records evidence provenance, operator action, timing, kit identity, image identity, review status, and export integrity.
- The Reference DB and classification thresholds require validation against kit-specific laboratory data before operational deployment.
- The prototype uses NIST hash/signature references for technical design but does not claim trusted timestamps, agency PKI, HSM protection, or legal admissibility.
- Legal compliance is a workflow responsibility shared with authorized officers and competent authorities; the app provides structured support, traceability, and certificate-ready exports rather than legal certification.
- Any production release must be reviewed against current Indian law, agency SOPs, kit-manufacturer protocols, forensic-laboratory validation, privacy requirements, and competent-authority approval.

## Detailed Screen Requirements

### Screen 1 - Login

- Badge ID and PIN fields.
- Optional biometric prompt.
- Prototype authentication may use seeded mock accounts, but PIN verification and biometric unlock remain separate concepts.
- On success, store `operator_id` in the session.
- Load Officer or Supervisor role.
- Officer routes to Home; Supervisor routes to Supervisor Review.
- Root/jailbreak status is a non-blocking prototype flag and is stored with the record when available.

### Screen 2 - Home

- Show officer name and badge ID.
- Start New Test action.
- View History action.
- Show synchronization state: SYNCED, PENDING, or FAILED.
- Route to New Test or History.

### Screen 3 - Kit Select and QR Scan

- Select from the bundled kit catalog, such as NIK Test A, B, C, and D.
- Scan a kit QR code.
- QR data includes kit ID, kit type, manufacturer, batch/lot, manufacture date, and expiry date.
- Scanning auto-fills metadata and locks the kit type selector.
- Optional Case ID or FIR number.
- Optional witness badge ID for future co-signing.
- Show kit type, batch, expiry, and VALID or EXPIRED status.
- VALID continues to the timer.
- EXPIRED or UNRECOGNIZED shows “Kit not valid - do not use” and blocks continuation.
- Persist kit metadata and validation status with the test record.

### Screen 3A - Reaction Timer

- Show the kit protocol checklist from the bundled Reference DB.
- START TIMER records `timer_start`.
- Required interval comes from the validated kit protocol and cannot be edited by the officer.
- Camera may open for alignment while the countdown runs, but capture remains locked.
- At the required time, vibrate and show CAPTURE WINDOW OPEN.
- Enable capture during the allowed window.
- If a maximum window exists and capture occurs after it, force Inconclusive.
- Store `timer_start`, `capture_time`, `elapsed_s`, `required_s`, and `in_window`.

### Screen 4 - Guided Capture

- Show a live camera preview.
- Overlay a Reference Card rectangle and Test Kit rectangle.
- Use camera capability detection for exposure and white-balance locking.
- Enable capture when alignment requirements pass.
- Run blur, exposure, clipping, and glare quality checks on capture.
- Laplacian variance below threshold produces “Image too blurry, retake”.
- Strong reaction-area reflection produces “Retake required - strong reflection detected”.
- Show an interim quality score, such as “92 / 100 - Good”, or Poor with retake reasons.
- Store capture quality reasons and retake count.

### Screen 5 - Mark Reference Patches

- Display the captured original image.
- Prompt in order: Tap WHITE patch, BLACK patch, RED patch, GREEN patch, BLUE patch, and GRAY patch.
- Store a five-by-five pixel average for every patch.
- Always allow Retake.
- Validate that all six patches are marked and inside the reference frame.
- Check expected hue for each patch.
- Check that white is not clipped and black is not crushed.
- Check the neutral black-gray-white ramp.
- Check calibration residual Delta E.
- Check that the card version is recognized in the Reference DB.
- All checks pass as CARD VALID and continue.
- Any failure becomes CARD INVALID, lists failed checks, and blocks continuation.
- Retake or End Test may save an Inconclusive result with reason Card calibration failed.
- Store card result and calibration Delta E.

### Screen 6 - Mark Sample Region

- Display the white-balance-calibrated analysis image; preserve the original separately.
- Prompt the operator to tap the center of the color-change area.
- Allow the circle radius to be adjusted with a slider.
- Convert the sample ROI median RGB to LAB.
- Reject a reaction area below the minimum size.
- Warn when ROI color spread indicates multiple colors.
- Hard-block strong reaction-area glare and require retake.
- Milder glare reduces quality and may contribute to Inconclusive.
- Uniformly pale or clear samples are not automatically treated as glare.
- Retake or End Test may save Inconclusive with the relevant reason.

### Screen 7 - Result

- Show Preliminary Positive, Preliminary Negative, or Inconclusive.
- Show confidence and Delta E.
- Show Capture Quality score and Good, Fair, or Poor band.
- Good is 80-100, Fair is 60-79, and Poor is below 60 for the initial prototype.
- Show top quality reasons.
- Show CARD VALID and calibration Delta E.
- Show kit type, batch, expiry, and validation status.
- Show required reaction time, actual timing, and in-window status.
- Show timestamp, GPS, operator ID, kit type, case ID, and Reference DB version.
- Show all applicable Inconclusive reasons.
- Recommended actions are Retake photograph, Repeat field test according to protocol, or Preserve sample for laboratory confirmation.
- Actions: Save, Retake, and Repeat test.

### Screen 8 - Save and Export

- Begin a single SQLite transaction.
- Persist the original image in the app evidence directory.
- Compute `prev_hash` from the previous record in the local chain.
- Canonicalize the record JSON.
- Compute `record_hash` over the documented canonical bytes.
- Sign the canonical record or record hash using the prototype device key.
- Store the public-key and signature metadata required by verification.
- Export JSON and image for verification.
- Generate the PDF with `react-native-html-to-pdf`.
- Embed image, metadata, hash, and QR code in the PDF.
- Commit the transaction only after the record and evidence references are consistent.
- Never physically delete a signed record; archive or tombstone it while preserving chain verification.

### Screen 9 - History

- Show newest records first.
- Show test ID, result, time, kit, sync status, and supervisor review status when available.
- Filters: date range, officer, result type, kit type, and case number.
- Open a read-only detail view equivalent to the Result screen.
- Warn that archival or tombstoning affects record visibility but does not remove signed evidence.
- Provide Repeat Test, creating a new linked record.

### Screen 9A - Retest Comparison

- Repeat Test opens the kit flow with case ID and kit type prefilled.
- A new kit scan is required.
- Save Test 2 as a separate record.
- Store `retest_of = Test 1 test_id` on Test 2.
- Show Test 1 and Test 2 side by side.
- Compare result, Delta E, quality, card status, kit/batch, reaction time, and timestamp.
- Do not merge results or compute a combined result.
- Test 1 is never edited.
- Each record keeps its own hash, signature, and chain position.

### Screen 10 - Verify

- Open `verify.html` in a browser or WebView.
- Accept the canonical JSON export and the original image.
- Recompute the original image SHA-256.
- Recompute the canonical record bytes and record hash.
- Verify the signature using the exported public key.
- Verify previous-hash links and chain integrity.
- Show separate results for image hash match, signature validity, record hash match, and chain status.
- Clearly label prototype signing and the absence of trusted timestamps or agency PKI.

### Supervisor Review

- Supervisor opens submitted records read-only.
- Review includes result, confidence, Delta E, capture quality, quality reasons, card status, kit details, timing, metadata, image reference, hash, signature state, and sync state.
- Supervisor selects REVIEWED, REQUIRES RETEST, or REQUIRES LAB CONFIRMATION.
- Review status is stored as a separate append-only review event or related record.
- The original signed test record is never edited.
- Officer History and Detail View show the review status when available.

## Canonical Data Model

### Test record

Required fields:

`test_id`, `created_at`, `timezone_offset`, `device_clock_warning`, `operator_id`, `operator_role`, `case_id`, `witness_badge_id`, `kit_id`, `kit_type`, `manufacturer`, `batch`, `manufacture_date`, `expiry_date`, `kit_status`, `reference_db_version`, `card_version`, `card_status`, `calibration_delta_e`, `timer_start`, `capture_time`, `elapsed_s`, `required_s`, `in_window`, `image_path`, `image_sha256`, `analysis_version`, `sample_roi`, `result`, `confidence`, `delta_e`, `inconclusive_reasons`, `recommended_action`, `quality_score`, `quality_band`, `quality_reasons`, `retake_count`, `retest_of`, `sync_status`, `previous_hash`, `record_hash`, `signature_algorithm`, `signature`, `public_key`, and `device_integrity_flag`.

### Location record

Store `latitude`, `longitude`, `accuracy_m`, `provider`, `acquired_at`, permission state, and an unavailable or low-accuracy state. Never fabricate a location when permission is denied or a fix cannot be obtained.

### Review record

Store `review_id`, `test_id`, `reviewer_id`, `review_status`, `reviewed_at`, and optional review notes as a separate append-only record or related table.

## State Machines

### Test workflow

`LOGIN -> HOME -> KIT_VALIDATED -> TIMER_RUNNING -> CAPTURE_WINDOW_OPEN -> IMAGE_CAPTURED -> CARD_VALIDATED -> SAMPLE_MEASURED -> RESULT_READY -> SAVED -> EXPORTED`

Failure or user actions may branch to `RETAKE`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `ENDED`. A failed hard gate must not continue to classification.

### Synchronization

`PENDING -> SYNCED` on successful adapter response; `PENDING -> FAILED` on failure; `FAILED -> PENDING` on retry. Local evidence is retained in every state.

### Review

`SUBMITTED -> REVIEWED`, `SUBMITTED -> REQUIRES RETEST`, or `SUBMITTED -> REQUIRES LAB CONFIRMATION`. Review transitions never mutate the test record.

## Classification and Quality Contract

- The original captured image is immutable evidence.
- Calibration operates on an analysis copy.
- Classification uses the versioned Reference DB, calibrated LAB values, Delta E, confidence, timing, and quality gates.
- The classifier must return a structured result, not only a display string.
- Hard gates override color classification and produce Inconclusive or retake-required behavior.
- A result must include the algorithm/reference version used so it can be reproduced.
- The prototype must use simulated colors and must never infer or display a specific controlled substance name from demo colors.
